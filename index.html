About

The bridge between the client and the code.

I'm an Implementation Engineer at Zino Technologies, where I own client delivery end-to-end. I sit with stakeholders to understand how their business actually runs, translate that into solution designs and integration specs, and then build what's needed to make it work — REST APIs, Python and SQL pipelines, validation rules and scheduled sync jobs.

My favourite kind of problem is a slow manual process hiding inside a big system. The warehouse network I worked on used to take a month to onboard a single site; after automating registration against SAP, it takes about three hours.

I hold a B.E. in Information Science from SDM Institute of Technology, and I care about clean data, clear communication and go-lives that don't need a firefight the next morning.
